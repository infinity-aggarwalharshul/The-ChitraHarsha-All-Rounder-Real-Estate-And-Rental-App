Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is AkashaAvas?

AkashaAvas is an all-in-one, AI-powered real estate platform by ChitraHarshaVPK Ventures. We help you find, buy, sell, or rent flats, apartments, and PGs across India, all while using the latest technology for security and personalization.

How is my data secured?

We use a multi-layered security approach, including quantum-level encryption, blockchain management for data integrity, and continuous dark web monitoring. Our systems are fully compliant with Indian Government data policies.

What is the "AI Assistant"?

Our AI Assistant is an advanced NLP (Natural Language Processing) model that can help you with your property search. You can ask it complex questions like "Find me a 3BHK apartment near a school in Koregaon Park with smart home features," and it will provide you with personalized results.

What is "The ChitraHarsha VPK Pay"?

It is our in-built, secure payment gateway for handling transactions, rent payments, and service fees. We also support cryptocurrency payments for eligible transactions.
